Jordan Governorates

Jordan is divided into 12 governorates, which are distributed across three main regions: the North, the Center, and the South.

Al-Karak

Al-Karak Governorate is one of the key governorates located in the Southern Region of Jordan. It is renowned for its rich history, strategic location, and prominent cultural heritage that plays a vital role in Jordan's traditional identity.

Geographically, Al-Karak is perched on a hilltop approximately 1,000 meters above sea level, offering breathtaking panoramic views of the Dead Sea valley. It is universally celebrated for Al-Karak Castle, a magnificent Crusader fortress that stands as an enduring symbol of resilience, architectural brilliance, and ancient battles.

Furthermore, the governorate is a vibrant hub of Jordanian traditions, famous for its warm hospitality, authentic folklore, and traditional dishes like Mansaf.

Al-Salt

Al-Salt Governorate is one of the distinctive governorates located in the Central Region of Jordan. It is globally renowned for its unique heritage architecture, historic yellow limestone Ottoman houses, and deep-rooted cultural harmony.

Geographically, Al-Salt is built across three prominent hills—Jadana, Al-Jad'a, and Al-Qal'a—surrounded by lush green valleys. It stands as a living museum of traditions, recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage site for its remarkable blend of history, architecture, and authentic Jordanian traditions.

Furthermore, the city is famous for its warm hospitality, traditional crafts, and delicious local delicacies like Mansaf and traditional sweets, making it an irreplaceable cultural landmark.

Ajloun

Ajloun Governorate is one of the most breathtaking governorates located in the Northern Region of Jordan. It is globally celebrated for its stunning natural landscapes, rolling green mountains, and dense oak and pine forests that make it a unique ecological haven.

Geographically, Ajloun is characterized by its high altitude and mild climate, offering refreshing breezes and picturesque views. It is universally famous for Ajloun Castle, a magnificent Islamic fortress built by Saladin's general in the 12th century, strategically perched atop a mountaintop to protect the region against Crusaders.

Furthermore, the governorate is a vibrant center for eco-tourism, rich biodiversity, traditional farming, and authentic Jordanian hospitality.
